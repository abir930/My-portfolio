Kazi Abir Hasan Embedded Systems • IoT Architecture
Ideas to Real World Solutions

I am Kazi Abir Hasan, a Computer Science student dedicated to the craft of embedded systems and Internet of Things architecture. My driving passion lies at the intersection of raw silicon and algorithmic intelligence: constructing machines and circuits that sense physical reality, process information deterministically, and actuate tangible change.

My engineering work spans robotics, sensor fusion, AI-driven embedded analytics, and hardware-software co-design. I believe that engineering achieves its greatest value when applied to physical problems: monitoring waterways for clean communities, streamlining traffic through real-time telemetry, or automating processes with high reliability.

Currently, I am investigating Edge AI integration on microcontrollers to perform intelligent inference directly on field hardware. I welcome collaborative opportunities, research partnerships, and engineering challenges.
